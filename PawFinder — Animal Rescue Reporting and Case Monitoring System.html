About Us

PawFinder is a GIS-based reporting and case-monitoring platform. Its mission is to support animal welfare by making it easy for community members to document animals in need. Reports are organized by location and condition and displayed on a map so that areas with many cases become visible. Community members can then discover animal welfare organizations nearby. PawFinder supports coordination only — it does not dispatch rescuers or guarantee that a rescue will take place.
